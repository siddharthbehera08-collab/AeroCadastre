## 4. Key Governance & Regulatory Facts

1. **Pre-Cadastre Compliance:**
   All candidate parcels generated by AeroCadastre carry the statutory status `NOT_ASSIGNED_PRE_CADASTRE`. Official 14-digit Bhu-Aadhaar (ULPIN) identifiers can only be assigned by state cadastral authorities upon ground demarcations.
2. **AI Council Adjudication:**
   The AI Council comprises 6 specialized autonomous agents (Vision, Geometry, GIS, ML, Anomaly, Field Verification). The Council is strictly advisory and does not claim legal adjudicative authority.
3. **Surveyor-First Philosophy:**
   AeroCadastre transitions cadastral surveyors from manual digitizers to evidence-backed verifiers, providing optimized inspection walking routes (TSP) and audit-safe copilot explanations.
