### B. Spatial Fusion & Inference Engines
- **Boundary Evidence Engine:** Generates candidate boundary vectors with distance, angle, and probability features.
- **Model E (Multi-Source Fusion):** 6-domain fusion engine with dynamic weight renormalization. Correctly handles missing layers without penalizing confidence. Tested across 8 edge cases.
- **Model F (Parcel Inference):** Planarization, polygonization, and feature enrichment generating `CANDIDATE_PARCEL` polygons with sliver filtering.

### C. Validation, Anomaly, & Adjudication Engines
- **Model G (Topology Validation):** Polsby-Popper compactness, self-intersections, gaps, overlaps, and automated topology repair.
- **Model H (GIS Conflict & Anomaly):** Detects building-road intersections, isolated structures, short fragments, and boundary disputes.
- **Deterministic Confidence Engine:** Multi-criteria Bayesian scoring engine with `HIGH`, `MEDIUM`, `LOW`, and `REJECT` categorization.
- **AI Council Multi-Agent Engine:** 6 autonomous domain agents (Vision, Geometry, GIS, ML, Anomaly, Field Verification) with strict precedence fusion.

### D. Production Backend, Ingestion, & Verification
- **Raster Ingestion & Bounds Validator:** `backend/gis/raster_ingestion.py` verifying formats, checksums, CRS, pixel size, and bounding boxes.
- **Model Adapters:** `experiments/adapters/model_a_adapter.py`, `model_b_adapter.py`, and `model_d_adapter.py` providing standardized inferential interfaces.
- **Pipeline Orchestration Service:** `backend/app/services/orchestration_service.py` managing project datasets, pipeline jobs, evidence, parcels, and anomalies.

- **Parcel History & Geometry Diff Engine:** `backend/gis/history_diff.py` computing symmetric difference area, IoU, Hausdorff boundary displacement, and centroid shift vectors between parcel versions.
- **Field Route Optimization:** `backend/gis/field_route_planner.py` metric TSP nearest-neighbor route planner with priority weighting.
